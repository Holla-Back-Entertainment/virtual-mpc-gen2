Hidden controls

Space play / stop   Pads: Z X C V = 1-4, A S D F = 5-8, Q W E R = 9-12, 1 2 3 4 = 13-16 (pad 1 bottom left, velocity 100). On screen, hit a pad near its centre for hard, near its edge for soft, then drag on it for pressure. Drag a knob or the dial faster for bigger steps. Arrow keys work on a focused knob, dial or the strip.

Stock firmware in a virtual machine. Works: touchscreen, main out sound, pads, buttons, data dial, Q-Link knobs (a turn counts while the knob is touched; the server adds the touch for dial and key turns), touch strip. Pad velocity and pressure follow where on the pad you hit. The Main volume knob sets this page's playback level (on the unit it is analog and never reaches the app). Layout and printed labels follow Akai's top-down product photo; which control name sits behind each printed label was checked against the running app (see panel/LIVE3-KEYS.md), and the keys marked as guesses there only match by name.

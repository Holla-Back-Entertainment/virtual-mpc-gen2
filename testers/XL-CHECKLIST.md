# MPC XL tester checklist

We built a virtual MPC XL panel from the stock firmware. We need a real XL to confirm which keys do what.
Time needed: 10 to 15 minutes. Nothing gets installed or changed on your unit.

## Before you start

1. Press Menu, then Preferences. Report your firmware version.
2. If it is 3.10.1, look for Preferences > Enhancements > Developer. Report if it is there or not.
3. Start a new empty project with one drum track. Do not load samples.
4. Take one straight top-down photo of the whole unit in good light.
5. Shifted functions are the red print. Hold Shift (either one) and press the key. Release both between steps.
6. If a step does nothing, that is a useful answer. Write "nothing".

## Steps

| Step | Do this | What to report |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Press Solo | Screen or light change? |
| 2 | Press Set | Screen or light change? |
| 3 | Press Audition | Screen or light change? Any sound? |
| 4 | Press Step 1 on the main screen, then Step 16 | Screen change? Which lights come on? |
| 5 | Press Save | Dialog? What does it say? Press Main to leave |
| 6 | Press Arrange (the one under Loop On, right side) | What shows? |
| 7 | Press Play, then hold Erase | Does the Erase light or screen change? Press Stop |
| 8 | Press Note Repeat, then hit a pad | Light, screen, repeating hits? Press it again to turn off |
| 9 | Tap Tempo four times | Does the tempo change on screen? |
| 10 | Press Copy | Screen or light change? |
| 11 | Press Quantize | Screen or light change? |
| 12 | Press Pre / post | Light change? Screen change? |
| 13 | Press USB Select | Does a message show? What does it say? |
| 14 | Press Q-Link | What screen opens? |
| 15 | Press Next Seq | What screen opens? Press Main |
| 16 | Shift + Copy | What happens? |
| 17 | Shift + Undo | What happens? (Redo?) |
| 18 | Shift + Save | What happens? Press Main to leave |
| 19 | Shift + Note Repeat | What happens? (Latch?) |
| 20 | Shift + Full Level | What happens? |
| 21 | Shift + 16 Levels | What happens? |
| 22 | Shift + Erase | What happens? |
| 23 | Shift + Tap Tempo | What opens? |
| 24 | Shift + Set | What happens? |
| 25 | Shift + Sample Edit | What opens? (Sampler?) |
| 26 | Shift + Pad Mute | What opens? (XY FX?) |
| 27 | Shift + Menu | What opens? |
| 28 | Shift + Loop On | What happens? |
| 29 | Turn the knob on the left panel, above Tracks / Outputs | What moves on screen? Which value? |
| 30 | Look for any light with no label next to it | Where is it, what colour, when does it change? |

## How to send results

Reply with the step numbers and a word or two for each, like "4 nothing, 5 save dialog".
Phone photos of the screen are fine. Add your firmware number from Before you start.

# MPC Live III tester checklist

We built a virtual MPC Live III panel from the stock firmware. We need a real Live III to confirm which keys do what.
Time needed: 10 to 15 minutes. Nothing gets installed or changed on your unit.

## Before you start

1. Press Menu, then Preferences. Report your firmware version.
2. If it is 3.10.1, look for Preferences > Enhancements > Developer. Report if it is there or not.
3. Start a new empty project with one drum track. Do not load samples.
4. Take one straight top-down photo of the whole unit in good light.
5. Shifted functions are the red print. Hold Shift and press the key. Release both between steps.
6. If a step does nothing, that is a useful answer. Write "nothing".

## Steps

| Step | Do this | What to report |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Press Set | Screen or light change? |
| 2 | Press Step button 1 on the main screen, then 16 | Screen change? Which lights come on? |
| 3 | Press Prev, then Next | Screen or light change? |
| 4 | Press Note Repeat, then hit a pad | Light, screen, repeating hits? Press it again to turn off |
| 5 | Press Play, then press Erase | Does the Erase light or screen change? Press Stop |
| 6 | Press Copy | Screen or light change? |
| 7 | Press Track Mute | Screen or light change? |
| 8 | Press T. Strip | Does a message show? What does it say? |
| 9 | Press Tap Tempo four times | Does the tempo change on screen? |
| 10 | Press Edit | What opens? |
| 11 | Press Q-Link once (bank 2). Touch Q-Link knob 1 | Which screen or overlay shows? |
| 12 | In bank 2, touch each of the 4 Q-Link knobs one at a time. Lightly, no turning | Anything odd? Any freeze? Do pads and keys still answer? |
| 13 | Still in bank 2, turn each of the 4 knobs one at a time | Does a value move? Does the panel freeze? |
| 14 | Press Q-Link until bank 1 comes back | Does it cycle through 4 banks? |
| 15 | Shift + Copy | What happens? (Delete?) |
| 16 | Shift + Undo | What happens? (Redo?) |
| 17 | Shift + Save | What opens? (Save as?) Press Main to leave |
| 18 | Shift + Note Repeat | What happens? (Latch?) |
| 19 | Shift + Full Level | What happens? (User level?) |
| 20 | Shift + 16 Level | What happens? |
| 21 | Shift + Erase | What happens? |
| 22 | Shift + Prev, then Shift + Next | What happens for each? |
| 23 | Shift + Load | What opens? |
| 24 | Shift + Notes | What opens? |
| 25 | Shift + Sample Edit | What opens? |
| 26 | Shift + Edit | What opens? |
| 27 | Shift + Main | What opens? |
| 28 | Shift + Sounds | What opens? |
| 29 | Shift + T. Strip | What opens? |
| 30 | Shift + Step button 1, then 2, then 3 | What happens for each? |

## How to send results

Reply with the step numbers and a word or two for each, like "4 nothing, 17 save as dialog".
Phone photos of the screen are fine. Add your firmware number from Before you start.
For steps 12 and 13, say clearly if the panel froze and how you got it back.
